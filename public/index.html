ABOUT THE DIRECTORY

A place to find your people.

This directory brings together independent working groups across the ontology engineering community. Hosting a listing does not imply ownership, governance, or endorsement of the listed organization.

Public sources add context and resources; each group’s detail panel identifies those sources.

Meeting schedules can change. Contact the organizers before attending. Contact information is public professional contact information supplied for this directory.

New listings are reviewed before publication. Suggest a correction from any group’s detail panel.

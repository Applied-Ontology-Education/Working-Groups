ABOUT THE DIRECTORY

A place to find your people.

This directory brings together independent working groups across the ontology engineering community. Hosting a listing does not imply NCOR owns, governs, or endorses the listed organization.

Seed descriptions and contacts were supplied by John Beverley. Public sources add context and resources; each group’s detail panel identifies those sources. “Contact organizers” means current membership eligibility has not been independently confirmed.

Meeting schedules can change. Contact the organizers before attending. Contact information is public professional contact information supplied for this directory.

New listings are reviewed before publication. Suggest a correction from any group’s detail panel.
